Ship experiments without the headache

A/B testing, analytics, and heatmaps in one lightweight script.

Scroll around and click a few things on this page — the tracking script records pageviews, clicks, mouse movement, and scroll depth for the Heatmap viewer, and buckets you into the "Hero headline" test (control vs. a bold red variant on #hero-headline).

Use the eye-icon preview link on a variation to force it instead of waiting on random bucketing, and check the Analytics tab after a few clicks on "Start free trial" (the test's goal).
